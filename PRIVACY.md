# Privacy Policy — Aptitude / מדיניות פרטיות

_Last updated: 2026-06-25_

This is a starter privacy policy for the **Aptitude** app. Review it with legal
counsel and host it at a public URL before submitting to the App Store (App
Store Connect requires a privacy policy URL for education apps).

## What we collect
- **Account data**: email and a username/display name you provide at sign-up
  (stored via Supabase Auth).
- **Learning data**: your answers, progress, streaks, XP and adaptive ability —
  used to run the app and personalize your learning path.
- **Push token** (optional): if you enable reminders, an Expo push token is
  stored to send the daily notification. It is kept separate from your public
  profile.
- **Analytics**: anonymous-by-default product analytics (PostHog) and error
  reports, used to improve the app. No analytics are sent if the app is built
  without an analytics key.

## What we do **not** do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not share your individual answers with other users. Leaderboards show
  only your username, display name, XP and streak.

## Data location
Data is hosted on Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, `eu-central-1`).

## Your rights
- **Access / export**: contact us to receive a copy of your data.
- **Deletion**: you can delete your account and all associated data at any time
  from **Profile → Delete account** in the app (this is immediate and
  irreversible). This satisfies Apple's account-deletion requirement.

## Children
The app is intended for users preparing for higher-education and placement exams.
